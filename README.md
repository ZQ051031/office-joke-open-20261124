One single-line office joke for the make.fun competition
"Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open".

The entry is a reply-all cascade: someone hits reply-all to beg
everyone to stop hitting reply-all, and the fix becomes the problem —
400 people are now replying-all to agree with him. The offence itself
is the punchline: agreeing requires the very act being protested.

## Entry

> Someone hit reply-all to say 'please stop hitting reply-all,' and now 400 people are replying-all to agree with him.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the joke to
`/output/entry.txt` — one line, no trailing newline, 116 characters.
